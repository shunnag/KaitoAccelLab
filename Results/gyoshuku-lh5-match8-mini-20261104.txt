Validation
- All 45 pointer/match8 pairs and all 45 match8/early4 pairs were byte-identical.
- Early4 recovered only about 1% on random while losing 2-3.5% on text/binary, and did not restore pointer-level random speed. It was rejected.
- Full pointer suite: 944 tests, 51 skipped, 0 failures; Results/gyoshuku-lh5-pointer-full-mini-20261104.txt.
- Logs: Results/gyoshuku-lh5-match8-ab-mini-20261104.txt and Results/gyoshuku-lh5-early4-ab-mini-20261104.txt.

The final adoption decision uses the adaptive variant, which keeps scalar matching until two matches of length 8 or longer have been found.
