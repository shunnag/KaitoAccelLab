RAR15 decodeNum / addBits inlining (2026-11-05)

Candidate
- Add @inline(__always) only to RAR15Decoder.decodeNum and addBits. getBits was already inlined by the optimizer.
- The threshold loop, packed-bit accounting, range checks and exact errors are unchanged.
- Generated-code inspection of the candidate found no decodeNum, getBits or addBits outline symbols; the baseline had an outlined decodeNum, and the first stage left addBits outlined.

Measurements
- Same RAR 1.54 document/audio archives as the consume experiment.
- kaito bench --threads 1, 50 extractions per timing, four alternating timings per executable.
- Stage 1 is decodeNum inline; stage 2 also inlines addBits.

M4 Max

baseline                   5.7425 ms   0.9390 ms

Mac mini

The final/original time reduction is about 15.1% for document and 20.4% for audio on M4 Max, and about 21.1% / 28.1% on Mac mini. The independent addBits stage improved a further 2.7% / 4.8% over decodeNum alone.
