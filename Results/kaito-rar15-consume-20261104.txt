RAR15 consume-only bit advancement (2026-11-04)

Candidate
- RAR15Decoder.addBits previously called bits.read(count) and discarded its value.
- Replace it with bits.consume(count), preserving the packed-bit budget check, actual bit count, alignment and overrun behavior while avoiding an unused peek/return and one duplicate bit-count validation.
- No other RAR decoder or format behavior is changed. The production change is one line in RAR15Decoder.swift.

Corpus and method
- The project's existing RAR 1.54 corpus in KaitoKit/inbox/rar15, including the 17-file doc_154_best.rar and audio_win_names_unpack15.rar.
- Independently built tracked-source baseline and one-line candidate; no third-party decoder source or disassembly was used.
- Scripts/benchmark_kaito_inprocess.py, kaito bench --threads 1, 50 extractions per timing, four alternating timings per executable. Archive-open time is excluded; entry decoding and CRC verification are included.
