Scalar-prefix variant
- A guarded variant compared the first 8 bytes scalar and used UInt64 only afterward.
- It recovered some random performance but lost most of the text improvement. For example, one text level 1 run was 145.180 MB/s, versus 166.380 MB/s for the unguarded median and 142.046 MB/s for pointer in the corresponding single run.
- The guarded change was rejected and reverted; the measured candidate is the unguarded implementation.
