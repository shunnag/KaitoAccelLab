PPMd state search unroll and threshold experiments (2026-11-04)

Candidates
- Unroll PPMdEncodingModel.find to inspect two states per iteration.
- Threshold variant keeps the original loop for contexts with at most 3 states and unrolls larger contexts.

- All 12 baseline/candidate output sizes were identical and the benchmark's independent oracles passed.

M4 Max unroll, median of three runs
- Binary improved 1.8-2.8%; text was within about 0.5% in either direction.
- Examples: binary H6 16.264 -> 16.711 MB/s (1.027x), binary I6 15.916 -> 16.350 MB/s (1.027x).

Decision
- Both variants helped binary but were not consistently faster on text. The threshold also reduced text H6 by 1.3%.
- Neither variant was adopted. PPMdModel.find was restored to the original implementation.
