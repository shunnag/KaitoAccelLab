Zstandard doubleHash repeat preload experiment (2026-11-04)

Candidate
- In ZstdParser.fastRepeats, calculate the three independent 8-byte history differences before extending any matching repeat.
- Preserve duplicate-repeat suppression, comparison order, strict longest-match selection, and the initial-history path.
- No other codec or level was changed.

Decision

- Not adopted. The experimental source change was individually reverted; GyoshukuKit has no remaining diff for this experiment.
