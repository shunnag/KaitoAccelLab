Zstandard paired dictionary insertion experiment (2026-11-04)

Candidate
- In fast and doubleHash match insertion, load two positions and calculate both hashes before storing them.
- Preserve the original insertion sequence and head/tag/short store order, including collisions. Keep a scalar tail for the final position.
- A temporary benchmark-only filter selected levels 1 and 3; default test behavior was restored after the experiment.

Validation
- Local debug ZstdEncoderTests: 21 tests, 3 skipped, 0 failures.
- The six initial level 1/3 frames were byte-identical to the original baseline.
- Five alternating before/after xctest runs: Results/gyoshuku-zstd-pair-insert-ab-mini-20261104.txt
- All 30 frame pairs (3 corpora x levels 1/3 x 5 rounds) were byte-identical according to cmp.

Decision
